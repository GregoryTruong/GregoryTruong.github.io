Exit 3Car Depreciation Analyzer汽车
Project · Machine learningPython · pandas · scikit-learn · matplotlib · pytest

When should you actually buy that car?

An end-to-end pipeline that ingests, cleans and merges used-car listings from two sources, then trains a gradient boosting model with monotonic constraints on age and mileage.

16,000+listings merged and cleaned
7.7%median error on price
R² 0.89on 3,294 held-out cars
~½yearly cost when bought at the best age, for 5 of 6 models
